What happens to a report why nothing counts until it's reviewed

1 · Logged as reported

Your words, kept as-is, marked patient-reported. Facts and interpretation never mix.

2 · Cross-checked

I compare it against communications, requests, and appointments already on file — supporting signals, conflicting signals, and what's missing.

3 · Verified or logged only

If the evidence holds, it becomes a tracked care gap with a responsible party and next step. If not, it stays on file as a report — nothing is discarded, nothing is overstated.
